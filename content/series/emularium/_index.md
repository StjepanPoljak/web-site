[Emularium](/projects/emularium/) emulates whole boards, not just chips. A
board is described as a KiCad schematic or a small TOML file; Emularium wires
up the parts, loads the firmware into the microcontroller and runs everything
in real time.

It started as a PIC16F876 emulator for my old Embedded Tetris project, which
is where this series begins.
